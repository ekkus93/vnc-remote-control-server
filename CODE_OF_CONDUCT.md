# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, publication of private information, threats, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Maintainers will investigate fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and apply proportionate corrective action, including warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion.
